Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Verified Leads respects privacy and is committed to handling personal information responsibly. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, stored and shared when you use our website or services.

1. Information We Collect

When businesses submit an order, we may collect:

  • Business category and business name.
  • Contact person's full name.
  • Telephone or WhatsApp number.
  • Business email address, if provided.
  • Business website or social media link, if provided.
  • Product or service descriptions.
  • Order, payment and transaction information.

We may also collect technical information such as browser type, device information and website usage data where applicable.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Process and manage customer orders.
  • Communicate about orders and delivery.
  • Verify payment transactions.
  • Provide customer support.
  • Maintain website security and prevent fraud.
  • Improve our services.
  • Meet applicable legal obligations.

3. Lead Information and Sharing

Verified Leads provides business lead-generation services. Lead information may include information voluntarily submitted by individuals who express interest in products or services.

Where individuals have been clearly informed and have given the necessary consent, their submitted information may be shared with the relevant business customer for the purpose explained when their information was collected.

We do not represent that information may be shared without the necessary legal basis or consent. Businesses receiving lead information are responsible for using it lawfully, respecting individuals' preferences and complying with applicable data protection requirements.

4. Payment Processing

Payments may be processed through NexaPay or another payment provider identified during checkout. Payment providers may process transaction information under their own privacy policies and terms.

Verified Leads does not request customers to submit payment card passwords or private wallet keys through our order form.

5. Information Sharing

We may share information with service providers where reasonably necessary to operate the website, process payments, provide hosting, maintain security or deliver our services.

Information may also be disclosed where required by law or where necessary to protect legal rights, prevent fraud or respond to valid legal requests.

We do not claim that personal information is never shared. Any sharing must have an appropriate legal basis.

6. Data Storage and Security

We use third-party technology providers, including Supabase, for database and application infrastructure where configured.

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or disclosure. However, no online system can guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including order administration, customer support, security and legal obligations.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and applicable legal requirements.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, individuals may have rights to:

  • Request access to their personal information.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion where legally applicable.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Withdraw consent where processing relies on consent.
  • Make a complaint to the relevant data protection authority.

9. Cookies and Website Technologies

Our website or its service providers may use cookies or similar technologies to support functionality, security and performance. Browser settings may allow you to control certain cookies.

10. Third-Party Websites

Our website may contain links to external websites and services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party websites. Please review their policies independently.

11. Children's Privacy

Our business services are intended for businesses and adults acting in a business capacity. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children without an appropriate legal basis and required permissions.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, practices or legal obligations change. Updated versions will be published on this page with a revised date.

13. Contact

For privacy questions, requests or concerns, please contact Verified Leads through the official business contact channel provided on our website.
